About Scale to Thrive

Scale to Thrive is a job-oriented training program for engineering students and recent graduates, run by Anvesana TBI and PES Trust. Two 12-week hybrid workshops, IT Skills and Advanced Excel with Corporate Readiness, combine hands-on labs, real projects and career preparation, and connect trained students with hiring companies.
